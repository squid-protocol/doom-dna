# `DOOM-E8` Amendment 1 (2026-11-22): reconciling two independent design reviews

Reviews: a Claude subagent and Gemini (`gemini-4-neon-high`), same brief, neither seeing the other's output. **both** = both reviews raised it. This amendment supersedes the sections of [E8_HALF_LIFE.md](E8_HALF_LIFE.md) it names; the rest stands. Status is still DRAFT until committed before any E8 scan.

## A. What changes in the question (both)

1. **H8.3 is split and narrowed.** H8.3(a) (age from the head alone) is **demoted to exploratory** and re-specified (D). H8.3(b) (divergence date between a child and its parent, with git ground truth) is **an exploratory feasibility check**, not a pass/fail claim. The sentence in §1 about identifying a port "by the divergence between two code lines over time" is reworded to: *estimating the age of a code line, and the fork date of a child from its parent, from snapshot survival.* No claim of general accuracy is made from four pairs.
2. **Prior knowledge (both).** E7 head-level numbers were seen before this design (structure low for Doom Retro at 0.3% of code; names overstate: 83% for Crispy and 63% for Retro; chocolate and crispy above the floor), and the 2005 to 2026 timeline of four ports in `METHOD.md` §10 was seen earlier. No E8 snapshot other than the 44 heads has been scanned or looked at. **The direction of H8.1 and H8.2 is therefore expected from E7 and weakly informative.** The informative parts are the *magnitudes* (the size of decay), *form* (smooth or burst) and *where it fails*.

## B. Independence, age, strata (both)

3. **Independent lineages.** Shared early history makes `crispy-doom`/`chocolate-doom`, `nugget-doom`/`woof` and `dsda-doom`/`prboom-plus` one observation counted twice. The **primary sample is the 11 non-overlapping lineages**: `chocolate-doom` (not `crispy-doom`), `woof` (not `nugget-doom`), `prboom-plus` (not `dsda-doom`), `doomretro`, `eternity`, `odamex`, `gzdoom`, `srb2`, `fastdoom`, `edge-classic`, `inter-doom`. Each excluded child is analysed only on its **post-fork segment** (child vs parent-at-fork), reported separately. Cross-validation is **leave-one-lineage-out**.
2. **Age and strata.** Age remains **years since the first commit of the local clone** (committer date, forced non-decreasing along first-parent history). Ports are tagged before scanning as **history-preserving** (their clone shares commits with another corpus port, or the repository is a documented continuation: `chocolate-doom`, `woof`, `prboom-plus`, `eternity`, `odamex`, `gzdoom`) or **import / fresh start** (`doomretro`, `srb2`, `fastdoom`, `edge-classic`, `inter-doom`). All tests are reported for both strata and the pooled sample; the stratum is fixed here, not after results. For import-style ports, the report also gives survival against **age since first appearance of linuxdoom code** (first snapshot with `surv_struct` at or above the floor).
3. **Reference.** `R1 = linuxdoom` is the **only** reference for H8.1 and H8.2. `R2` (declared parent at its 2026 head) is used **only for post-fork snapshots**, or as the parent **at the same commit date** where the parent's history exists; comparing a child's 2005 code against a parent's 2026 head is not scored. Ports whose real code line is Boom, MBF or ZDoom (`gzdoom`, `edge-classic`, `odamex`, `eternity`) form a stratum expected to be low and flat against `linuxdoom`, reported separately.

## C. Snapshots (both; replaces §2 snapshots, §6 sensitivity)

4. **De-duplicate snapshots by SHA within a port** before any trend or fit; tied fractions are one point.
5. **"Earliest usable snapshot"** (at least 100 eligible functions) replaces the hard-coded `f = 1/32` in every rule; `f = 0` is reported separately.
6. **A precondition table is produced before scanning** (committed with the plan): per port, number of distinct snapshots and an eligible-function count at each planned SHA from a cheap `ctags` count (not an outcome). **H8.2 is declared untestable for any port with fewer than 4 usable snapshots**, and for the whole experiment if fewer than 8 lineages qualify.
7. **A commit-count plan** (same fractions of first-parent commits) is generated and committed with `e8_plan.csv` before scanning, for the 11 lineages only (about 40 extra scans); it is a registered sensitivity.
8. **Scope audit.** Before scanning, `git ls-tree` at every planned SHA records which `scope.csv` directories exist (Heretic, Hexen, Strife and the rest); the pruned file set per snapshot is logged. Sensitivity: exclude snapshots whose pruned layout differs from the head's.
9. **Language shares.** Each snapshot records the share of eligible functions by language (C, C++, other). A snapshot with a shift of at least 10 percentage points from the head's share is flagged; sensitivities restrict to pure-C snapshots and to the `portable` lens. (E7 showed structure collapsing across C to C++ while names stay: this alone can mimic H8.1 and H8.2.)

## D. Measurement and tests (replaces §3 thresholds, §4, §5)

10. **Threshold policy (Gemini).** Twin thresholds are per snapshot (`fdr 0.01`, as E7) for pairwise and head-only quantities (`H8.3`), and **pooled across a port's snapshots** (`twins.pooled_threshold`) for within-port trends, because `METHOD.md` §10 documents per-snapshot thresholds flipping a port between 16% and 3% year to year. **Both are reported; the pooled one is primary for H8.1 and H8.2.**
11. **Names** use the E7 stoplist and decoy certification for `surv_names`, not only for `shared`.
12. **Duplicates.** The primary analysis keeps duplicates as E7 does (so E8 inherits E7's known defects: generic-key rule, no de-duplication, vendored code kept; Amendment 2 for E7 is pending). A **de-duplicated sensitivity** (one representative per exact-digest group on both sides) has equal standing: a conclusion that changes under it is called fragile.
13. **Statistics.** Per-lineage Kendall `tau-b` between age (years) and survival, two-sided, with ties from duplicate SHAs removed. With 4 to 7 snapshots a single port rarely reaches p < 0.05 (for 4 points the smallest possible two-sided p is 0.083), so **per-port significance is not used as evidence**; per-lineage `tau` and the amplitude `Delta = surv(earliest usable) - surv(head)` are the units, with **function-level bootstrap** (as E7) for each snapshot's survival, never resampling time points.
14. **Primary endpoints, Holm-corrected across these three:**
    * **P1 (structure trend):** one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on the 11 lineage `tau`s for structure, H0 median `tau >= 0`.
    * **P2 (structure vs names):** paired Wilcoxon on `tau_struct` against `tau_names` across the 11 lineages, H1 `tau_struct < tau_names`.
    * **P3 (names flat):** equivalence: the 95% bootstrap interval for the median `Delta_names` lies entirely inside `[-0.10, +0.10]`.
    Effect-size criteria are reported **separately** and are not part of the inferential tests: median structure `tau` at most -0.6; structure `Delta` at least +0.10 in at least 70% of the scored lineages. **Power statement (registered):** on 11 lineages an exact sign test reaches p < 0.0125 only at 10 of 11 or more; the Wilcoxon tests are used for their power, and a sign-test result is secondary. Everything else (H8.3, functional form, burst, post-fork segments, controls) is descriptive.
15. **Functional form (descriptive, pinned).** Constrained NLS on `S(a)`: model 1 `S0 * exp(-lambda * a)` with `0 < S0 <= 1`, `lambda >= 0`; model 2 `S_inf + (S0 - S_inf) * exp(-lambda * a)`; uncertainty by function-level bootstrap per snapshot. **Burst** is rate-normalised: `max_k (dS_k / dt_k) / (S_range / t_range)`, computed also on an evenly spaced yearly grid (not on the half-life grid, where the long last interval makes steps unequal).
16. **H8.3(a), exploratory, re-specified.** Estimand: *age of the code line since the port's first commit*, from **self-origin survival** `surv(s_0 -> head)` with `s_0` the earliest usable snapshot (so `S0 = 1` by construction), `lambda` from the other lineages (leave-one-lineage-out). `S_head` at or below the floor is **censored** (reported as unestimable, neither success nor failure), `S_head >= 1` gives age 0. Compared against **two baselines**: the leave-one-out median age, and a rank regression of age on head survival. **The error metric is MdAPE** (median of `|a_hat - a| / a`). The model is called to add information only if it **beats both baselines** (paired Wilcoxon on absolute error) and its Spearman exceeds a **permutation null** that shuffles ages across lineages. Because ages and head survival are already correlated at the heads (E7), a high Spearman alone is not evidence.
17. **H8.3(b), exploratory, re-specified.** Pairs: `crispy-doom`/`chocolate-doom` (ground truth **2013-12-09**, the last shared first-parent commit; `timeline_events.csv` records 2014-03-04, Crispy's first own commit; both are listed, the git-shared date is the scoring truth), `dsda-doom`/`prboom-plus` (2020-11-09), `nugget-doom`/`woof` (2021-08-31), `kocoa-doom`/`mochadoom` (2019-07-20; cross-language, reported separately). `mood`/`linuxdoom` is excluded (78% duplicates). Grid: **semi-annual** steps over the full overlapping span, fixed before scanning. Pre-fork pairs are the same commit by construction and are excluded from the fit. Estimator: a **hinge (broken-stick)** model fitted on post-fork points only, the fork estimate being where the post-fork curve extrapolates back to `S = 1`; both `shared` (max) and a **symmetric/min survival** are reported (so a child that only adds code still registers). No ±1-year pass rule: the error in years is reported per pair and per method with a baseline (fork = midpoint of the overlap), and a **placebo** (the same procedure on the parent at randomly shifted dates and on child/unrelated-port pairs) gives the chance level.
18. **Controls (replaces §4 controls).** Keep `helion` and `rust-doom` as from-scratch DOOM rewrites with the **E7 expectation**: structure below the floor; names may attach (E7: `helion` 0.06 under names), so no names prediction is made. `freedoom` is kept as a descriptive asset check only. **Add** large unrelated C/C++ histories (`nginx`, `curl`, `libuv`; full history to be fetched if not on disk) across the same half-life grid against `linuxdoom`, and a **placebo reference** `R0` = an unrelated old C project (`nginx` at its E7 pin) against every DOOM-port snapshot: any apparent trend of survival with age against `R0` is a pipeline artefact (growth, duplicates, loc distribution) and is reported.
19. **Name-blind text arbiter.** `measure/e7_arbiter.py` (E7) is run against `linuxdoom` on the unique snapshots of the 11 lineages (about 70 commits) as a descriptive calibration curve beside `surv_names` and `surv_struct`, so a drop in structure can be told from a measurement artefact.
20. **Measurement noise.** Two ports are scanned twice (all their snapshots) to characterise engine determinism before any trend is read.
21. **Partial correlation.** Survival vs age is also fitted controlling for `log(eligible functions)` of the snapshot (repo growth changes collisions and ratio-test crowding in opposite directions).
