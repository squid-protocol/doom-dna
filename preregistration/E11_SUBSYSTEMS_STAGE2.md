# DOOM-E11 stage 2: how much of `linuxdoom`'s call-graph architecture does each port keep, beyond what copying guarantees?

**Status: RECONCILED; becomes REGISTERED by the commit that adds this file in this form, before any placement or metric is computed.** Two independent design reviews (a Claude subagent and Gemini `gemini-4-neon-high`) are reconciled in §G. Stage 1 ([results](../results/e11/E11_STAGE1_RESULTS.md)): Louvain finds stable, meaningful groups ("subsystems") in `linuxdoom`'s call graph (19 groups, 16 of 10+ functions, ARI 0.78, NMI 0.52 with the hand taxonomy). Scanner checkout `b1a9e92` and engine `704cb35f0` (E7 pins name sfs `bcd3cb7`; the matching code is identical).

## A. Question, and what this can and cannot say
Do ports keep `linuxdoom`'s subsystem architecture, measured against chance, with placement that uses neither names, file paths, nor the call graph being measured? **Placement:** structural twins (name-blind function fingerprints, E7 Amendment 2 pooled K = 25 cutoff, either direction), strictly one-to-one on both sides; a port function takes its twin's group.
**What a high result means for a same-language port:** mostly that copied code kept its calls. Both reviewers showed a random 15% sample of `linuxdoom`'s own functions already scores z 14 to 18 on a shuffle-only modularity test, so a shuffle-only test cannot fail. This stage therefore (1) measures the **fraction of the pure-copy expectation** each metric reaches, (2) tests whether **new calls** (edges gained) respect the imported group boundaries, which copying does not guarantee, and (3) tests whether the groups remain communities of the port's **full** call graph, dominated by thousands of added functions. **Translations are out of scope** (no usable twins; call-graph-aligned placement imposes the reference's shape; see §F for what a null there would and would not mean).

## B. Design
* **Reference partition:** `linuxdoom` call graph (undirected, as stage 1), Louvain, **iterative consensus** (Lancichinetti and Fortunato 2012: 100 partitions, co-association threshold 0.5, re-cluster until stable), at **resolution 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0, all co-primary**. Unstable nodes (no stable partner) are excluded from the reference and counted. The 43 isolated functions are excluded before matching. The coverage denominator is the clustered `linuxdoom` functions (736 at γ = 1.0), with 779 total and E7-eligible reported.
* **Programs, fixed now from E7 Amendment 2's pooled shares with `linuxdoom`, before any placement:** **primary scored set (pooled share at least 0.30):** `chocolate-doom`, `crispy-doom`, `doomgeneric`, `sdl-doom`, `doom-ascii`, `doom-wasm`. **Secondary (0.05 to 0.30), reported with flagged low power:** `fastdoom`, `inter-doom`, `doom-ios`, `prboom-plus`, `dsda-doom`, `woof`, `srb2`, `mood`, `puredoom`, `mochadoom`, `eternity`, `odamex`. **Controls (enumerated, all reported with their placed counts):** `nginx`, `curl`, `lua`, `leveldb`, `libuv`, `quake`, `quake2`, `helion`, `rust-doom`, `room4doom` and the remaining Phase A programs. A program is **scored** when it has at least **100 placed functions and 300 calls among them**; M3 needs at least **100 directed cross-group calls**. Every program below a floor is listed with its numbers as insufficient, never as zero.
* **Placement:** twins port to `linuxdoom` in both directions; the union, then **strict one-to-one**: a port function matched to several `linuxdoom` functions in different groups, or a `linuxdoom` function matched by several port functions, is dropped as ambiguous and counted. Reported per program: eligible, placed, ambiguous, coverage of the reference.
* **Ports and clustering of the port:** the port's own consensus partition is computed on the **full port call graph** (Louvain, 30 seeds, consensus at 0.5), then restricted to the placed functions. Isolated placed functions are excluded from every metric and counted.

## C. Metrics (effect sizes with bootstrap 95% intervals over placed functions; no z scores; no pooled p across ports)
* **E1 edge retention (primary):** of the directed calls among matched `linuxdoom` functions, the share that exist between their twins in the port. **E1g gained-edge fidelity (primary):** among calls in the port between placed functions that are not copies of a `linuxdoom` call, the share that stay within an imported group, against a size- and degree-matched null (random pairs among the placed functions with the same group margins). This is the measure of extending the architecture that copying does not guarantee.
* **M2 (primary):** **adjusted mutual information (AMI)** between the imported labels and the port's own consensus partition restricted to the placed functions; homogeneity and completeness reported (a port whose groups refine the reference's scores high on one and low on the other). **Ceiling:** the median AMI of each of the port's 30 seed partitions (restricted) against its own restricted consensus. **Null:** label permutation, and the **file-level permutation** (below).
* **M1 (descriptive):** modularity of the imported labelling on the port's induced graph among placed functions, reported raw and as a **fraction of the pure-copy expectation** (the same labelling on `linuxdoom`'s induced graph for the matched functions with edges dropped at the port's retention rate and random edges added to the port's density; 200 draws).
* **M3 (descriptive, scored only above the M3 floor):** cosine of the directed off-diagonal group-to-group call matrix of the port's placed functions against `linuxdoom`'s matrix restricted to the matched functions; gap against a **degree-stratified** label shuffle.
* **Pure-copy curve:** for each placed fraction (0.1 to 0.6) the metrics for a perfect copy of `linuxdoom` sampled at random, giving the reference line and the power at the floors (the sampling test the reviewers ran, made a registered output).
* **Controls and references:** (i) **file-level permutation null:** each `linuxdoom` source file moved intact to a random group, sizes preserved, 1,000 draws (replaces the within-file permutation, which leaves most labels unchanged); (ii) **file-label reference:** each placed function labelled by its twin's source file; if files explain the port as well as groups, the subsystem claim adds nothing beyond file layout; (iii) the hand taxonomy (file-name prefix) as an alternative reference; (iv) unrelated programs and from-scratch rewrites placed the same way.
* **Per-subsystem retention:** share of each group's functions placed and retained.

## D. Predictions (before any placement)
* **P1 (E1):** edge retention is at least **0.50** for each of the six primary ports. (If lower, the one-to-one twin set is too ambiguous for the rest to mean anything.)
* **P2 (M2, primary):** AMI of the imported labels against the port's own consensus partition is above the label-permutation 95th percentile for all six, and above the **file-level permutation 99th percentile** for at least five.
* **P3 (M1, exploratory):** M1 reaches at least 0.8 of the pure-copy expectation for at least four of six.
* **P4 (E1g, primary):** gained-edge fidelity exceeds the matched null for at least four of six (the ports extend inside the imported architecture).
* **P5 (control, expected to be uncomfortable):** the file-label reference explains the port's partition about as well as the subsystem groups (AMI within 0.05) for at least three of six; if true, the groups add little beyond file layout and that is reported as the result.
* **P6 (controls):** every unrelated program and from-scratch rewrite is insufficient (under 100 placed functions); none is scored. If one is scored, its AMI is below its file-level permutation 99th percentile.
* **P7 (robustness):** the sign and significance of P2 hold at all three resolutions for at least five of six.
* **P8 (sanity):** coverage ranks the programs as E7 Amendment 2's pooled shares do (Spearman at least 0.8).
M2 relative to its ceiling is **reported, with no pass/fail threshold** (the draft's 50% had no basis); P3's 0.8 is a stated guess from the copy-dominated core, not a derived value. Failed predictions are reported as failed. The headline is descriptive: per port, coverage, edge retention, M2 against ceiling, gained-edge fidelity, and whether subsystems add anything over files.

## E. Limits
Same-language ports only. Twin placement favours distinctive, larger functions (exact-clone bypass at loc 12 or more), so tiny glue wrappers that carry cross-subsystem calls are under-placed and M3's cross edges are a biased subsample; endpoint-size distributions are reported against the reference edges. Coverage near the twin cutoff cliff can be bimodal for threshold reasons (E7 Amendment 2 §A). Louvain's group count depends on resolution (11, 16, 24); hence the co-primary sweep. The graph is undirected for clustering and directed for call matrices. Third-party source is not committed.

## F. What translations would need, and what a null there would mean (conditional follow-up, not a claim of this stage)
Two defensible routes, neither tested: (a) a transparently **non-blind name- and path-placed arm** paired with the file-level permutation null (extends `wiring-against-chance.md` without graph alignment); (b) **blind placement from data and constants** (unique literals, lump names, tables, angle and fixed-point constants), orthogonal to names and call edges, validated first on same-language ports where twins give the truth. A zero gap for a translation would not separate "architecture lost" from "functions mis-placed" unless the placement has been validated; any call-graph-aligned placement is excluded.

## G. Reviews and reconciliation
**Adopted from both:** the shuffle-only modularity z cannot fail and z grows with N, so it is dropped for effect sizes against the pure-copy expectation; the within-file permutation control is a near-identity and is replaced by a file-level permutation and a file-label reference; M2 uses AMI (NMI has a high random floor) with the port's Louvain run on the full port graph and a ceiling computed on the restricted set; the placement floor is fixed from E7 coverage before metrics and all dropouts are reported; one-to-one on both sides; coverage denominator and isolate handling; directed call matrices with a separate M3 floor; the consensus rule made explicit; resolution sweep co-primary; predictions P1, P3, P5, P7 of the draft rewritten (several could not fail); the pin discrepancy recorded.
**Adopted from the Claude review:** the copy-and-perturb baseline and the pure-copy curve; edge retention and gained-edge fidelity as primary; degree-stratified null; bootstrap intervals over placed nodes; per-port reporting with no pooled p; the tiny-function under-placement caveat.
**Adopted from the Gemini review:** per-subsystem retention; the data-and-constants route for translations; sweeping resolution on the port as a sensitivity.
**Declined:** dropping M1 entirely (kept as descriptive, as a fraction of the pure-copy expectation); a pass/fail threshold for M2 against its ceiling (none has a basis; reported only).
