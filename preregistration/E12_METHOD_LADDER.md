# DOOM-E12: why match, then extend? A method ladder for function matching against `linuxdoom`

**Status: RECONCILED; becomes REGISTERED by the commit that adds this file in this form, before any matching or scoring.** Two independent design reviews (a Claude subagent and Gemini `gemini-4-neon-high`) are reconciled in §H; the first draft is replaced because both showed its rungs were not comparable (different populations, different numbers of emitted pairs, a degenerate calibration rung) and its truth was mishandled. Engine `704cb35f0`, scanner `b1a9e92`.

## A. Why this experiment, and what is already known (disclosure)
The Seed-and-Extend method came from a chain of follow-ups in `followups/`: name-blind fingerprint twins work within a language (`chocolate-doom` 420 twins at 90.5% name agreement) and find **nothing across languages** (0 twins for Go, Scheme, Dart); the composition lens and offset calibration gave small gains; seeding anchors and following call-graph neighbours gave large gains on a favourable anchor set; the production method then matched functions with **name precision** (not recall) of 67 to 95% in same-language ports, 76.6% for Go, with unrelated programs at 0 to 0.8%. **What was never done is the ladder on one footing.** **Prior knowledge, stated plainly:** `results_all.csv` already holds seeds and extension results for 22 of the programs below; `cross-lang-twins` holds fingerprint results for six ports; the scanner's blind-placement study was tuned on `chocolate-doom`, and the published seed fraction (15%), calibration gate (cosine 0.70, size ratio 0.20) and coherence-gate thresholds were set on it and after the fact. The following are **new here**: matched-volume comparison, the graph-only and measurements-only baselines, the calibrated-versus-uncalibrated extension ablation, the fingerprint threshold sweep, the text truth, and the held-out controls for the gate.

## B. Question
For function matching against `linuxdoom`, what does each ingredient add, **at equal numbers of emitted pairs**, and where does each stop working?

## C. Design
* **One scoring universe and one rule for all rungs:** code functions with at least 6 lines on both sides (E7's eligibility); matchers may use the full call graph (minimum 3 lines) but a pair with an endpoint under 6 lines is dropped from emission and counted. Every rung emits a **strict one-to-one list, ranked by a confidence score, with ties broken by (score, port function size, row index)**; all randomness seeded.
* **Rungs** (the matcher code is the published `followups/seed-and-extend/measure.py` and the scanner's twin search, ported to this engine; nothing is tuned):
  * **F0 fingerprints alone:** structure lens twins, pooled K = 25 cutoff (E7 Amendment 2), either direction, one-to-one; headline setting FDR 0.01 and ratio 0.8 fixed now; a **descriptive sweep** over FDR {0.001, 0.01, 0.05, 0.10} and ratio {0.7, 0.8, 0.9, off}. Rank: distance, exact clones first.
  * **F1a, F1b, F1c (cumulative cross-language presets, each one change):** portable lens; plus composition keyword mode; plus within-repository **mid-rank** normalisation.
  * **G0 measurements only:** descriptors without call-graph roles, optimal transport (no graph), reciprocal best hits ranked by coupling mass.
  * **G1 call-graph shape only:** the nine call-graph roles only (no measurements), optimal transport, same.
  * **G2 fused:** fused Gromov-Wasserstein (descriptors plus hop distances), reciprocal best hits ranked by coupling mass.
  * **S seeds:** the published Tier-1 rule, the top 15% of G2's hits (a point on G2's ranking, reported as such).
  * **E1, E2, E3 extension:** S extended by 1, 2, 3 hops, **uncalibrated** (offset 0); and **E3c** the published method (offset calibrated from the seeds). Ranking for the combined list: seeds first by coupling mass, then each extension pair by hop and proposal score.
  * **Offset calibration is tested here only as E3c against E3**, not as a fingerprint rung: the draft's fingerprint calibration rung is dropped because it has no anchors where it matters (0 twins for Go, Scheme and Dart) and mean-shift followed by rank normalisation is a no-op.
  * **Gate row (descriptive, L7):** the figure builder's coherence gate (`strong_fgw >= 0.20`, `mean_sup >= 2.10`) applied to E3c, **pinned to the committed `build_subsystem_map.py` of the `subsystem-map` branch** (formula and thresholds as committed), evaluated on **held-out controls** (`leveldb`, `curl`, `libuv`, which are not in the scored control set below) and on the SL programs.
* **Programs, fixed now (functions under 10,000; the larger `gzdoom`, `doomsday`, `doom3-bfg` are a stated scope limit):** **SL (same language):** `doomgeneric`, `sdl-doom`, `doom-ascii`, `doom-wasm`, `puredoom`, `mood`, `fastdoom`, `doom-ios`, `prboom-plus`, `woof`, `crispy-doom`, `inter-doom`, `doomretro`, `nugget-doom`, `dsda-doom`, `eternity`, `odamex`, `edge-classic`, `srb2`. **`chocolate-doom` is the development port**: reported separately, excluded from every count in the predictions, and its results are development evidence only. **T (translations):** `gore`, `doom-scheme`, `dartdoom`, `mochadoom`, `managed-doom`, `kocoa-doom`. **Controls:** hard negatives `lua`, `nginx`; scratch rewrites `room4doom`, `rust-doom`; same-studio `quake`, `quake2` (reported apart); held-out for the gate `leveldb`, `curl`, `libuv`.
* **Truth, none of it used by any rung.** **SL:** the E9 name-blind text arbiter pairs against `linuxdoom` (`pair_class` vertical, **`eligible_primary` ignored**, since it is 0 by construction for these), mapped to engine rows with the loss per program reported; duplicate bodies handled as **equivalence classes** (`dup_rep`): an emitted pair is true if it joins members of the same classes as a truth pair. Three truth variants are always reported: Jaccard at least 0.9, at least 0.6, and identifier-normalised at least 0.9. Unmapped or unlabelled functions are **never counted false**; precision is reported as "true / emitted", "unlabelled", and the lower bound. **Recall is function-level**: the share of port functions that have at least one truth partner and are matched to any of their partners. Recall is stratified by Jaccard band and function size, since the arbiter and fingerprints both reward unchanged code. **T:** the pinned E7 name key and stoplist, a noisy key reported separately; plus a **blind audit** of 40 randomly drawn emitted pairs per translation (`gore`, `doom-scheme`, `dartdoom`, `mochadoom`), rated by model raters against both function bodies with rung and name hidden (disclosed as model raters), to estimate the name key's precision. **Controls:** every emitted pair is false except for the same-studio programs, which are reported apart.
* **Metrics:** each rung is a precision-recall curve over its own ranking; the **comparison point is recall and precision at matched volume N = 50, 100, 250, 500** (and at each rung's full list); rungs that emit fewer than N are compared at their full list and flagged. For controls: **emitted pairs per rung and false pairs per 1,000 functions at each N**. **Null:** each graph-using rung (G1, G2, S, E1 to E3c) is also run on a **degree-preserving rewiring of the port's call graph** (10 draws, programs of at most 3,000 functions plus `chocolate-doom`), giving what the method manufactures from degrees and measurements. **Intervals:** cluster bootstrap resampling source files, then programs; paired differences only for recall at matched N (shared truth denominator); precision comparisons by curves with their own intervals.

## D. Predictions (before any matching; chocolate-doom excluded from counts)
* **P1a (primary):** F0 at the registered setting has precision at least 0.90 against the Jaccard 0.9 truth on at least 15 of the 19 SL programs.
* **P1b (primary):** at N = 250 (or F0's full list if shorter), E3c's function-level recall exceeds F0's by at least 0.05 on at least 10 of the 19 SL programs, with E3c precision at least 0.75 at that N.
* **P2 (primary):** on `gore`, `doom-scheme` and `dartdoom`, F0 and F1c each emit fewer than 5% of the program's eligible functions at the registered cutoff, and E3c reaches name-key precision of at least 0.50 at N = 250 on `gore`.
* **P3 (primary):** at N = 100, G1 (call-graph shape only) has precision at least 0.30 below G2 on at least 14 of the 19 SL programs.
* **P4:** calibration adds under 0.05 of recall at N = 250 (E3c minus E3) on at least 5 of the 6 T programs.
* **P5:** extension costs precision: from E1 to E3 at N = 250 precision falls by at most 0.15 absolute on at least 14 of the 19 SL programs.
* **P6 (primary, controls):** at N = 100 the extension rungs (S, E1 to E3c) emit at least 90 pairs on every hard-negative and scratch-rewrite control (documenting that extension emits regardless of truth), so false pairs per 1,000 functions are high; and the **gate (L7) rejects all five held-out and hard-negative controls and accepts at least 17 of the 19 SL programs**.
* **P7:** the ordering of rungs by recall at N = 250 has Kendall's tau of at least 0.8 between the Jaccard 0.9 truth and the Jaccard 0.6 truth.
Primary: P1a, P1b, P2, P3, P6; no multiplicity correction is claimed (counts, not tests). P2's F0 half and P4 restate published results and are marked as known; **P1b, P3, P5, P6's gate half and P7 are new.** Failed predictions are reported as failed.

## E. Limits
The text truth is biased toward unchanged code and cannot reward matching of heavily edited functions (the stratified recall shows this); translation truth is a noisy key audited on a small sample, so translation precision is a lower bound; no text truth exists for translations at all. The study covers one family of programs and omits the three largest engines. The development port shaped the published gates, so "no tuning" means no tuning here. Third-party source is not committed.

## F. Reporting
Per program and rung: pairs emitted, dropped endpoints, precision (three truths), unlabelled, function-level recall (and by band and size), matched-N table, controls' emission and false-pair rates, rewiring null, gate outcomes, truth-mapping loss, the audit sample result; rung-order table; predictions scored.

## G. Not done
No blind-wiring or architecture claim; no ranking of programs by ancestry; no gate threshold is tuned.

## H. Reviews and reconciliation
**Both agreed (adopted):** rungs emitted very different numbers of pairs (about 100 for seeds, about 500 for extension), so comparison is at matched N with PR curves and a stated confidence for every rung; the calibration rung was degenerate (no anchors for Go, Scheme, Dart; rank normalisation is shift-invariant) and calibration is now tested as E3c against E3; L3 to L4 changed features and selection together, so G0, G1, G2 and S are separated, with S as a budget point on G2; the text truth is many-to-many (329 of chocolate-doom's 796 matched port functions have several partners) and needs equivalence classes and function-level recall; it is easy-biased, so recall is stratified; `eligible_primary` is 0 for every vertical pair and is ignored explicitly; the name key is lenient and noisy and is audited; quake and quake2 are same-studio and stratified; P1 mixed two claims of different strength and is split; P2 conflated a known and an unknown half; P4 was trivial where there are no anchors; P5's monotonicity was unfalsifiable (extension only adds pairs) and is replaced by the precision cost; P6's gate needed held-out controls and a pinned formula; chocolate-doom, which shaped the published gates, is excluded from the counts; the post hoc status of the published numbers is disclosed (§A); hard SL ports (`odamex`, `eternity`, `edge-classic`, `srb2`, `dsda-doom`) and the other large-truth ports (`crispy-doom`, `inter-doom`, `doomretro`, `nugget-doom`) are added.
**Adopted from the Gemini review only:** one scoring universe (loc at least 6) across rungs and a strict one-to-one tie rule; mid-ranks; the three-step cumulative fingerprint presets; a degree-preserving rewiring null for graph-using rungs; the correction that the published 67 to 95% is precision, not recall; a cluster bootstrap by file.
**Adopted from the Claude review only:** the explicit stratified recall by Jaccard band and size; the unlabelled column and the lower-bound statement; the blind audit of the name key.
**Declined or limited:** sparse FGW for the three largest engines (stated scope limit); Holm correction (counts, not tests).
