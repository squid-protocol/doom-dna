# E17 (DRAFT v1, 2026-11-27): full lesion screens of divergent ports (Mocha Doom, gore) and what they add

**Status: DRAFT; needs two independent reviews, a dated reconciliation and a commit before any screen outcome is read.** Builds on E15 (screens of chocolate-doom, managed-doom, prboom-plus: faithful ports) and E16 (symptom diagnosis). The two engines were built and validated without reading outcomes (Mocha: zero-lesion replay 77/77, classical-rebuild equivalence 20/20, clean digests deterministic; gore: pending).

## Why
E15 found that for faithful translations, clean call-count profiles identify partner functions as well as or better than lesion phenotypes (N0), so lesions' added value was not shown. The question that remains is whether phenotypes add information where the ports are **not** faithful. Mocha (Java; matches chocolate-doom's digests on only 49 of 77 demos) and gore (Go; diverges on 69 of 77) are such ports. Each lesion is compared with the port's **own clean digest** (self-differential), so divergence from chocolate-doom does not enter the lesion outcome.

## Design
* Same lesion kinds, ids, exclusions (shift and index lesions out of the primary analysis), hot and inert classes, demos (the 77), runner and analysis pipeline as E15, unchanged; per-port frozen definitions in E15 apply. Screens are run one port at a time under the shared-box rules (max 3 workers, niced).
* **Partner definition (frozen before outcomes):** function pairs between each new port and chocolate-doom by (i) the E12 structural matches (twin tiers and extension tiers) and (ii) name agreement after prefix stripping. A pair is **name-agree** if (ii) holds and the name is unique in both ports. Call-count identity across ports is no longer required.
* **Clean baselines for every pair:** call-count profile distance computed from each port's own clean entry counts on the 77 demos (N0, as in A4), and the E7 text arbiter where available.

## Hypotheses (primary unless marked)
* **H-B1 (primary): lesions add information where call counts cannot decide.** On name-agree pairs of each new port with chocolate-doom, partner recovery by the lesion phenotype distance (E15 D) is **at least 0.10 higher in top-5 (paired cluster bootstrap over functions, lower bound > 0)** than recovery by the call-count profile distance, in the pooled Mocha and gore pairs; E15's faithful-port gap (N0 at least as good as lesions) is the contrast. Reported separately for each port; the pooled test is the primary.
* **H-B2: lesion-effect families recur.** At least 8 of the 11 E15 lesion-effect families have a profile in each new port within 0.4 (E15 metric) of the chocolate-doom family profile.
* **H-B3 (descriptive, with audit):** name-agree pairs whose phenotype distance exceeds the E15 5th-percentile threshold of random cross-port pairs are listed and audited by two reviewers; the share confirmed as real role differences (not noise, not hot-or-inert artefacts) is reported.
* **H-B4: SFS confidence.** The size-aware confidence score from the SFS calibration keeps **AUC >= 0.75** for predicting behavioural confirmation (D at or below the 5th percentile and the same call-profile class) on the new ports' structural twin and extension pairs.
* **H-B5 (E16 follow-up, descriptive):** diagnosis with the chocolate-doom dictionary of Mocha lesions at the family-removed condition: top-5 against the baselines, in the more divergent port (the transplant upper bound is E16's T1).
* **Controls:** shuffled partners (chance), within-function split-half ceiling (PC1f), the E15 gates PC1g (cross-port reproducibility of hot and inert classes), and a ceiling from identical-text pairs where they exist.
* **Pre-declared null:** if H-B1 fails, phenotype distance is not distinguishable from call counts even for unfaithful ports at this resolution, and the lesion programme's value is reduced to the calibration data and families.

## Limits
Two divergent ports are two points; Mocha is itself the source of Kocoa; gore diverges on most monster demos, so its screen measures lesion effects on code that already fails monster behaviour (reported by demo family); Kocoa (Kotlin, fully divergent but unscreenable without a Kotlin engine and Freedoom loading) is deferred.

## v2 (2026-11-27): reconciled after two independent reviews (Claude subagent; Gemini, `results/e16/E17_review_gemini.txt`). Where v2 differs from v1, v2 governs.
**Disclosures.** (a) E15 and E16 outcomes are known to the authors (E15: families, the 5th-percentile cutoff, N0 near-perfect on faithful ports; E16: gore and Kocoa symptom summaries), so the **direction** of the hypotheses is not blind; only the Mocha and gore lesion outcomes are. (b) Clean-digest facts are known: Mocha matches chocolate-doom on 49 of 77 demos; gore diverges on 69 of 77 (median tic 2, 1 crash, 2 hangs). (c) N0 matching has **not** been computed on the Mocha or gore clean profiles. (d) The Mocha screen was **launched 2026-11-27 17:49 before this reconciliation was committed**; the lesion set, demos and runner are unchanged by it, no outcome has been read (row counts only), and the analysis code is frozen before any outcome is read. (e) The Mocha engine's crash path (an uncaught exception) was not exercised before the launch (the first outcomes will show it; a crash-only lesion count is reported).
**Order and stopping.** Mocha is the **sole confirmatory port**. Gore runs only after (i) its engine passes the same validation as Mocha's (zero-lesion replay, 20/20 classical equivalence, determinism) and (ii) a pre-outcome clean-coverage viability check finds at least **40 reachable name-agree functions**; otherwise gore is dropped. Gore is analysed **separately, never pooled** with Mocha, and is descriptive. In-flight abort rule for either screen: pause if crash plus hang exceeds 15% of the first 500 jobs, or Xvfb or the runner fails; aborted runs are archived unread. Hang re-run timeout 60 s.
**H-B1 (confirmatory, Mocha only).** Queries run **Mocha to chocolate-doom** over the same 140 usable chocolate-doom candidates (fixed pool; the reverse direction is a sensitivity). Truth: the **strict set** (name-agree and an E12 twin tier or a text-arbiter confirmation) is primary; the loose set (name-agree only) is a sensitivity; pairs where either function is hot, inert or has fewer than 4 observed demos are listed and counted as unrankable (not a miss), with the 4x4 usable/hot/inert/<4 transition matrix reported. **Comparators:** raw-L1 N0 and scale-invariant N0 (cosine and Spearman on log1p counts over co-observed demos; binary reach Jaccard), each computed on (a) all 77 demos and (b) only the demos where Mocha matches chocolate-doom (49) with counts truncated at Mocha's first divergence; a **combined ranker (D plus N0 by rank mean)**; lesion distance D with the missing-D (fewer than 5 co-observed demos) rate reported, plus per-component ablations (kill fraction only, first tic, class). **Pass rule:** D top-5 minus the best N0 variant's top-5 has point lift >= 0.10 **and** a 95% cluster-bootstrap lower bound > 0 (clusters = chocolate-doom functions, 10,000 resamples), **and** D top-5 >= max(3 x chance, 0.5 x PC1f), **and** at least **40** usable strict pairs (else "inconclusive for power"). **Difference in differences** versus the managed-doom gap on the same chocolate functions is reported with its bootstrap interval. Also reported: "counts plus lesions beats counts alone" (combined vs best N0), the lift on N0-tied or missed pairs, the name-only baseline, E12 structural distance as a matcher, shuffled partners within strata of function size and reach, MRR, top-1 and tie sizes (pessimistic).
**Gore stratification (descriptive).** Primary phenotype uses only demos gore reaches and replays without a tic-0/1 start-state artifact; crash and hang demos excluded; results stratified by demo faithfulness (match, early or late divergence) and family (bot vs monsters-on, shareware vs Freedoom, sticky, chaos).
**H-B2 (descriptive).** The 11 E15 lesion-effect families are frozen by the E15 commit; "profile in the new port" = the port's lesion-effect profile for the family's matched functions (1-to-1 name-agree); null = a shuffled-demo profile; reported with the E15 0.4 cutoff and the null side by side, no pass/fail count.
**H-B3 (descriptive with audit).** Flagged pairs: name-agree strict pairs whose D is at least the **25th percentile of random cross-port D** (looks as unlike as an ordinary random pair); up to 40 audited blind by two reviewers (Claude and Gemini) with a written rubric; Cohen's kappa reported.
**H-B4 (descriptive).** The SFS confidence score formula is frozen at the E16-era commit; AUC with a function-clustered CI and **within-tier** AUC (twin and extension separately), power gate 40 pairs.
**H-B5 (descriptive).** Uses E16's FROZEN_SCORER.json with the >= 3-demo filter and its baselines, test-half chocolate-doom targets, counterparts = name-agree without the identical-profile condition; stated as exploratory.
**Controls and gates (corrected).** PC1f (within-function split-half reliability) and PC1g (E15: within-port split-half group agreement, AMI >= 0.40) are **hard gates** for H-B1 on Mocha as in E15; the "identical-text ceiling" of v1 is dropped (does not exist across languages). **Uninterpretable outcomes (pre-declared):** a PC1f or PC1g failure; fewer than 40 usable strict pairs; missing-D (fewer than 5 co-observed demos) imputation above 40% of pairs; crash plus hang above 15%; hub or tie dominance; a result carried by one demo family. **Null wording:** "not distinguishable at this resolution" only when the lift's 95% interval lies within +-0.05; otherwise "inconclusive".
**Lesion generation per port.** Per-port lesion mix, compile-loop drop counts and lesions per function are reported (Mocha: 5,934 kept, 599 dropped, 258 functions); per-lesion-class sensitivities as in E15; "index lesions" are excluded by construction in the Java engine (array index lesions are never generated).
