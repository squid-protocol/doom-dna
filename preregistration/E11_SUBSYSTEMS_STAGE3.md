# DOOM-E11 stage 3: is `linuxdoom`'s subsystem partition specifically predictive of other programs' call-graph communities, from measurements alone?

**Status: RECONCILED; becomes REGISTERED by the commit that adds this file in this form, before any placement or metric.** Two independent design reviews (a Claude subagent and Gemini `gemini-4-neon-high`) are reconciled in §G; the first draft is replaced because both showed its outcome could not separate DOOM's partition from generic software structure. Follows [stage 1](../results/e11/E11_STAGE1_RESULTS.md) and [stage 2](../results/e11/E11_STAGE2_RESULTS.md).

## A. Question and honest expectation
Stage 2 could only follow `linuxdoom`'s subsystems into ports that keep structural twins. Stage 3 places **every function of every program** on a reference partition **from its measurements alone** (no names, paths, strings, file grouping or call edges) and asks: **does `linuxdoom`'s call-graph partition predict a program's own call-graph communities better than partitions made the same way that have nothing to do with DOOM's design?** The earlier sfs blind-placement study found measurements alone say little across languages (measures-only balanced accuracy 0.10 to 0.26 on translations) and that call-graph-using methods manufacture agreement. So the **expected** outcome is that this works for close and modified C ports and perhaps Go, and is uninterpretable elsewhere; the design's job is to say which, with controls that tell when a positive is meaningless.

## B. Design
* **Placement (arm F, primary):** sfs `blind.place`, method `features` (k-nearest reference functions, k = 15, sfs default; sensitivity k = 5 and 30), **geometry-only descriptors** (size, complexity, arity, vocabulary and decision density; no signal densities, no archetype, which the earlier ablation showed add little and can leak API vocabulary), each column rank-normalised within its repository so a language's offsets cancel, **no graph roles, no smoothing, so no call edge is read**. Decision rule **`lift`** (vote over the subsystem's reference share; `argmax` collapses 45 to 90% of functions into playsim in the earlier study) is primary; `argmax` is reported, and conclusions are called robust only if both agree. **Abstention:** a function whose nearest-reference distance exceeds the 95th percentile of `linuxdoom`'s own leave-one-out nearest distances is unlabelled (so port-unique code is not forced onto a subsystem); coverage is reported; sensitivity at the 90th and 99th percentiles.
* **Reference partitions:** the DOOM partition is `linuxdoom` iterative-consensus Louvain (stage 2 code) at resolution 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 (co-primary). **Alternative references (the specificity controls):** (a) **20 random partitions** of `linuxdoom`'s functions with the same group-size profile; (b) **3 graph-blind 16-means clusterings** of `linuxdoom`'s descriptors (different initialisations); (c) the **consensus Louvain partition of each other available program** (`nginx`, `curl`, `lua`, `leveldb`, `libuv`, and the Phase B unrelated programs found in the E7 scans), at the resolution whose count of groups of 10+ functions is closest to 16, each used as a reference to place the port's functions the same way. Reference functions without a stable group are excluded and the retained fraction reported.
* **Outcome X (excess homogeneity):** for each program and each reference, `homogeneity = 1 - H(label | community) / H(label)` computed over **communities of at least 10 functions** of the program's own call graph (undirected Louvain, resolution 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 swept on the port side; 10 seeds each; seed spread reported), over non-abstained functions; **`X_ref` = homogeneity minus its mean under 1,000 global label permutations** (so references with different numbers of groups are comparable). Homogeneity is invariant to a port subdividing a reference group into several communities, unlike AMI, and restricting to communities of 10+ removes the singleton noise. A program is scored only with at least **300 functions and at least 10 communities of 10+ functions** at that resolution; smaller programs (for example `doomf77`, the COBOL toys, `fsharp-doom`, `cssdoom`) are listed and not scored.
* **Specificity (the registered result):** for each program, the **percentile of `X_DOOM` among the alternative references' `X`** (about 30 references), and the difference `X_DOOM - median(X_alt)`; bootstrap interval over the port's functions. "DOOM-specific" means `X_DOOM` exceeds all but at most 5% of the alternatives (percentile at least 95).
* **Calibration (gate, on the reference itself):** **leave-one-out** placement of `linuxdoom`'s own functions from the others (arm F, same abstention), scored against its own consensus labels: accuracy, balanced accuracy (subsystems with at least 5 functions), Cohen's kappa against a label-shuffle null, 95% intervals. **Gate: median-over-folds kappa at least 0.20, with the lower bound above the shuffle null's 95th percentile.** Basis: the earlier measures-only ablation reached kappa of about 0.2 on the closest translations; leave-one-out on the reference should do at least as well. A fail is reported as "arm F carries too little to support any port-level claim". Twin-derived labels (stage 2) on the close copies are reported as a **secondary sanity check only**, not a gate: twin-placed functions are the easy near-identical ones.
* **Circularity arms (descriptive, quantifying manufactured agreement):** **G** (adds nine call-graph roles), **S** (F plus label propagation over the port's call graph), **W** (fused Gromov-Wasserstein, alpha 0.5, programs of at most 3,000 functions only). For each arm, X is computed on the real graph and on **100 degree-preserving rewirings with placement rerun on each rewired graph** (as in `blind.py`), giving the agreement the arm manufactures from measurements and degrees alone. Arm F is run on the rewirings too (its labels do not change, but the communities do). No pass/fail; this reports how much of each arm's X is the method.
* **File control:** X computed with each function's **source-file group** (files merged to the DOOM partition's number of classes by size) as the label set, per program: the agreement that file cohesion alone gives, to be read beside arm F.
* **Programs and groups, fixed now:** **close copies (C):** `chocolate-doom`, `crispy-doom`, `doomgeneric`, `sdl-doom`, `doom-ascii`, `doom-wasm`. **Modified or derived (M):** `mood`, `prboom-plus`, `woof`, `dsda-doom`, `inter-doom`, `fastdoom`, `doom-ios`, `eternity`, `odamex`, `srb2`, `puredoom`, `nugget-doom`, `doomretro`, `gzdoom`, `doomsday`, `edge-classic`, `doom3do`, `doom3-bfg`. **Translations (T):** `gore` (Go), `doom-scheme`, `dartdoom`, `managed-doom`, `mochadoom`, `kocoa-doom`, `doomf77`, `doo-cr`. **From-scratch rewrites (R):** `helion`, `rust-doom`, `room4doom`, `cssdoom`, `fsharp-doom`, `doom-fx`, `sqldoom`. **Toy raycasters (K):** `agentic-cobol-doom-cc`, `agentic-cobol-doom-codex`, `fps-cob`, `c-raycasting`, `c-raycasting2`, `goom3d`. **Unrelated (U):** `nginx`, `curl`, `lua`, `leveldb`, `libuv`, `bubbletea`, `macroquad`, `carddemo-cobol-ctrl`. `quake`, `quake2` (same studio) and `freedoom` (build tooling for assets, not a rewrite) are reported separately. Each program's group is fixed here; none is reassigned after a result.
* **Inference:** per-program percentile and interval, not pooled; group comparisons by the rank-based effect size (probability that a random program of one group has a higher specificity percentile than one of another group). Only P1 to P4 are primary; the rest are descriptive. No pooled p across programs.

## C. Predictions (before any placement)
* **P1 (gate):** leave-one-out kappa of arm F on `linuxdoom` meets the §B gate.
* **P2 (primary, close copies):** for at least five of the six C ports, `X_DOOM` is DOOM-specific (percentile at least 95) at the primary setting (reference resolution 1.0, port resolution 1.0).
* **P3 (primary, false-positive rate):** among the scored U programs, at most one of them is DOOM-specific (about the 5% a null would give).
* **P4 (primary, Go):** `gore` is DOOM-specific; the other scored translations are each reported (no registered prediction beyond: at most three of the remaining six are DOOM-specific, since the earlier study found weak signal for Java, Kotlin, C# and Fortran).
* **P5 (modified ports, fills stage 2's gap):** at least four of the five of `prboom-plus`, `woof`, `dsda-doom`, `inter-doom`, `fastdoom` are DOOM-specific.
* **P6 (dose-response):** Spearman correlation between specificity percentile and the ordinal group rank (C = 3, M = 2, T = 1, R and U and K = 0) over scored programs is at least 0.5.
* **P7 (shared task):** R and K are not DOOM-specific more often than U (reported with the effect size; the earlier study found rewrites at chance).
* **P8 (robustness):** P2 holds at all three port resolutions for at least five of six, and under `argmax` as well as `lift`.
Failed predictions are reported as failed. The circularity arms and the file control are reported without predictions.

## D. Limits
Leave-one-out calibration is on one program; there is no ground truth for translations, so a DOOM-specific result for a translation means its measurement-defined classes align with its own communities in DOOM's way better than alternative partitions do, which can reflect shared task or file organisation as well as ancestry; R, K and U separate part of that. Eight unrelated programs (Phase A and B scans) is a small control set; more would sharpen it. Measurements predict subsystem weakly across languages (earlier study), so a null for a translation is **not** evidence of lost architecture. Louvain communities depend on resolution (swept). Third-party source is not committed; code and engine (`704cb35f0`) and scanner (`b1a9e92`) commits are recorded.

## E. Reporting
Per program: functions, scored or not and why, coverage after abstention, `X_DOOM`, percentile among the alternatives, interval, spread over seeds; per group effect sizes; the calibration table; the circularity table by arm; the file control; the retained-reference fractions.

## F. What stage 3 does not do
It does not compute blind wiring (shown in the earlier study to be manufactured by the method), does not claim ancestry, and does not call any program's architecture "preserved" or "lost"; it reports whether DOOM's partition is specifically predictive of that program's own structure.

## G. Reviews and reconciliation
**Both reviews agreed:** the draft outcome (AMI of arm-F labels against the port's own communities minus a rewired-graph null) is not specific to DOOM, because in any program similar-looking functions share files and call one another; the rewired null does not remove that and destroys legitimate structure; AMI between a handful of labels and hundreds of communities is size-biased and noisy; the calibration gate on twin-placed functions is biased toward easy clones, the 0.40 threshold was unjustified, and arm F (measurements only) is weaker than the sfs main method and was not evaluated there; the five-program `max(U)` bar is fragile; the circularity test had an undefined null for the graph-using arms, an unrunnable arm W for the larger unrelated programs and an invalid converse; forcing a label on port-unique code and the playsim collapse under `argmax` are problems; several predictions were postdictions, trivially passed, or lacked thresholds; unspecified pipeline choices (decision rule, k, isolates, seeds, tuning) had to be fixed.
**Adopted:** reference-swap specificity (random partitions, graph-blind 16-means, other programs' partitions) as the registered result; homogeneity over communities of 10+ functions, null-adjusted, replacing AMI; per-program percentiles and intervals, group effect sizes, only P1 to P4 primary; leave-one-out calibration on the reference with a justified threshold, twin labels demoted to a sanity check; geometry-only descriptors, `lift` primary with `argmax` reported, distance-based abstention, port-side resolution sweep; circularity arms with placement rerun on rewired graphs (100 draws), arm W capped and described, converse dropped; the file-label control; reclassification (`freedoom` out, `edge-classic`, `doom3do`, `doom3-bfg` to M, `mood` to M, the toy raycasters to K); minimum program size; a dose-response prediction.
**Declined or limited:** expanding the unrelated set to 12 to 15 programs by new scans (only the already-scanned unrelated programs are used, stated as a limit); a coverage-versus-X curve beyond the three abstention thresholds.
