# DOOM-E11 stage 3 (DRAFT, not registered): does `linuxdoom`'s subsystem organisation travel to ports that changed a lot, or were translated?

**Status: DRAFT 2026-11-23. Becomes REGISTERED when two independent design reviews are reconciled in a dated section and this file is committed before any placement or metric.** Follows [stage 1](../results/e11/E11_STAGE1_RESULTS.md) (call-graph communities of `linuxdoom` are stable and meaningful) and [stage 2](../results/e11/E11_STAGE2_RESULTS.md) (they persist in close copies, where twin placement works; modified ports and translations were unscored for lack of placed functions).

## Question
Stage 2 could only place functions that have a structural twin in `linuxdoom`. Here every function of every program is placed on `linuxdoom`'s discovered subsystems **from its measurements alone** (size, complexity, arity, vocabulary and decision density, signal densities, archetype; no names, paths, strings or file grouping), and the placed labels are compared with the port's **own** call-graph communities. Does a function that looks, by measurement, like `linuxdoom`'s subsystem X land in the same community as the other functions that look like X? Is that more than unrelated programs show?

## What is already known (sfs blind-placement study, engine `6c7c6c1bc`, `examples/doom/blind_placement.md`)
Using the hand taxonomy as reference and a name/path answer key, blind placement worked for close translations (Go kappa 0.50, Dart 0.31, Scheme 0.26), weakly for Java, Kotlin, C# and Fortran (0.03 to 0.17), at chance for reimplementations; **rerunning on a degree-preserving rewiring of the call graph kept much of the placement accuracy (for example 0.60 to 0.39 for Go)**, and blind **wiring** failed as a check (every port, including reimplementations and COBOL raycasters, scored 0.91 to 0.96 because who calls whom is predictable from degree and size). That study's verdict: placement says close translations have DOOM's layout; it gives no independent wiring number. **Stage 3 therefore does not compute blind wiring.** It uses a different outcome (below) and tests the circularity directly.

## Design
* **Reference labels:** `linuxdoom` iterative-consensus Louvain groups (stage 2 code), resolution 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0, all co-primary; unstable reference functions excluded.
* **Programs and groups, fixed now:** **close copies (C):** `chocolate-doom`, `crispy-doom`, `doomgeneric`, `sdl-doom`, `doom-ascii`, `doom-wasm`, `mood`. **Modified ports (M):** `prboom-plus`, `woof`, `dsda-doom`, `inter-doom`, `fastdoom`, `doom-ios`, `eternity`, `odamex`, `srb2`, `puredoom`, `nugget-doom`, `doomretro`, `gzdoom`, `doomsday`. **Translations (T):** `gore` (Go), `doom-scheme`, `dartdoom`, `managed-doom` (C#), `mochadoom` (Java), `kocoa-doom` (Kotlin), `doomf77` (Fortran), `doo-cr`. **From-scratch rewrites (R):** `helion`, `rust-doom`, `room4doom`, `cssdoom`, `fsharp-doom`, `doom-fx`, `sqldoom`, `freedoom`. **COBOL and other raycasters (K):** `agentic-cobol-doom-cc`, `agentic-cobol-doom-codex`, `fps-cob`, `doom3do`, `doom3-bfg`, `edge-classic`. **Unrelated (U):** `nginx`, `curl`, `lua`, `leveldb`, `libuv`; `quake`, `quake2` reported separately (same studio).
* **Placement arms** (sfs `blind.place`, portable lens, descriptors rank-normalised within each repository so a language's offsets cancel; k = 15 as in sfs; nothing tuned): **F (primary):** measurements and archetype only, no graph roles, no smoothing, so **no call edge is read**; **G:** adds nine call-graph roles (degrees, depth, PageRank); **S:** F plus label propagation over the port's call graph (0.3, three steps); **W:** fused Gromov-Wasserstein alignment (alpha 0.5), run only for programs with at most 3,000 functions. Arms G, S, W exist to measure how much agreement the call graph manufactures.
* **Calibration with truth (gate):** on the seven close copies, the blind labels (each arm) against the twin-derived labels from stage 2, on the twin-placed functions: accuracy, balanced accuracy, Cohen's kappa against a label-shuffle null. **Gate: median kappa of arm F at least 0.40 over the seven.** Below it, translation results are reported as uninterpretable.
* **Outcome E (agreement with the port's own wiring):** for each program, the port's call graph (undirected) is clustered by Louvain (30 seeds, resolution 1.0); `AMI(blind labels, community)` is averaged over seeds. **Null:** the same labels against communities found on **degree-preserving rewirings** of the port's call graph (10 draws; the labels depend only on measurements in arm F, so they are unchanged); `E = AMI_real - AMI_rewired_mean`. Also the label-permutation AMI (chance floor). E asks: do the measurement-defined classes align with the port's actual wiring beyond what degrees alone produce?
* **Controls and bars:** E is compared across groups. The bar for "stands out" is the **maximum E among U** (clean unrelated) and, separately, among R and K (same task, so a shared-domain effect is possible and is itself a finding). Also reported on the **highest-confidence quarter** of functions (vote concentration; informative in the sfs study).
* **Per-subsystem breakdown:** which of `linuxdoom`'s groups carry the agreement (for example, playsim-like classes).

## Predictions (before any placement)
* **P1 (gate):** median arm-F kappa on the seven close copies is at least 0.40.
* **P2:** E for every close copy exceeds the maximum E of U.
* **P3:** E for `gore` exceeds the maximum E of U and of R.
* **P4:** E for `mochadoom`, `kocoa-doom`, `managed-doom` and `doomf77` does not exceed the maximum E of R (at least three of four).
* **P5 (circularity):** for the unrelated programs, E under arm S and arm W is larger than under arm F in at least four of five (graph-using placement manufactures agreement); if F is as large as S, circularity is not the issue and that is reported.
* **P6:** modified ports (`prboom-plus`, `woof`, `dsda-doom`, `inter-doom`, `fastdoom`), unscored in stage 2, now have E above the maximum of U in at least four of five.
* **P7:** conclusions P2, P3 and P6 keep their direction at all three resolutions.
* **P8:** `quake` and `quake2` (same studio) are not above the maximum of U by more than the spread within U.
A failed prediction is reported as failed. The headline is descriptive: E by group, with controls.

## Limits
Every function receives a label even when it has no counterpart in `linuxdoom` (port-unique code is forced onto one of its subsystems; confidence and the high-confidence quarter partly address this). Shared task (a renderer is a renderer) can produce agreement in rewrites and raycasters without ancestry; groups R and K separate that from U. AMI between 16 labels and hundreds of communities is small in absolute terms; only comparisons against the rewired null and across groups are interpreted. Louvain communities depend on resolution; the port side is fixed at 1.0 and the reference side swept. The portable lens, k and arms follow sfs defaults. Third-party source is not committed.
