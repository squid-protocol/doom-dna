# DOOM-E13: behavioural conformance of DOOM engines under deterministic replay, and a differential localiser

**Status: DRAFT 2026-11-26. Becomes REGISTERED when two independent design reviews are reconciled in a dated section and this file is committed before the registered runs (Parts A and B below are read only after that commit).** Harness: `demo/harness/`; tool: `demo/harness/difflocalize/`.

## 0. What this is, and what has already been seen (disclosure)
E13 began as an exploratory programme (notes: `docs/notes/DEMO_STUDY_PLAN.md`; per-demo tables `demo/phase*.csv`). **Already seen, so not predictions here:** the conformance results of 14 engines on three demo sets (three stock demos; 300 "sticky" random demos, seed 20261125; 200 "chaos" demos, seed 777; 47 bot demos), including: the six C engines, Managed Doom (C#) and doom-ascii matched the reference on every demo; Mocha 290/300 sticky and 191/200 chaos; room4doom 45/300 sticky, 1/200 chaos, 12/47 bot (47/47 once the demo-header "no monsters" byte is rewritten to 1); puredoom 52/300 and 2/200 (first difference in the weapon field); Kocoa and gore 0 on every monsters-on set (gore 17/47 on bot demos); the root cause of gore's first failure (a loop-index bug in `p_CrossSubsector`; fixing it delays its median first divergence from tic 6 to tic 104). Also seen: a 12-mutant smoke run of the localiser (5 detected; hits mixed), which motivated adding variant B (callers) below. **New here:** (A) the localiser's validity on injected bugs, and (B) replication of the conformance table on held-out demo seeds.

## A. Differential localiser: validity on injected bugs
**Tool (tier 1).** Given a reference engine and a subject engine and one demo: (1) first diverging tic and digest field; (2) the first game tic whose sequence of gameplay-RNG calls, labelled by the calling function, differs, and the callers present in only one engine (the *symptom functions*); (3) *suspects* = the symptom functions plus their static callee closure from the scanner's call graph (`function_data.calls_out_to`), to depth D. **Variant A:** callees only. **Variant B:** also seed with the symptom functions' callers up to two levels. When there is no RNG difference, a fixed field-to-function-family prior (`FIELD_FAMILY` in `locate.py`, frozen at this commit) seeds the closure instead.
**Test set.** 120 random mutants of the reference engine (chocolate-doom, 16 simulation files: p_enemy, p_map, p_maputl, p_sight, p_mobj, p_user, p_pspr, p_inter, p_doors, p_floor, p_ceilng, p_spec, p_switch, p_plats, p_lights, p_telept), seed 20261126; one change per mutant (relational, equality, `+`/`-`, `&&`/`||`, or integer constant + 1). A mutant is **detected** if any of 33 demos (20 sticky, 10 chaos, the 3 stock demos) differs from the reference engine on any compared field; the localiser runs on the demo with the earliest divergence. The mutated function is known from the scanner's database. Mutants that do not build or are never detected are reported but not scored.
**Predictions (made before the 120-mutant results were read).**
* **M1 (descriptive):** between 30% and 60% of buildable mutants are detected.
* **M2 (primary):** among detected mutants, the mutated function is in the variant-A suspect set at depth <= 3 in at least **50%**, and at depth <= 6 in at least **65%**.
* **M3 (primary):** the median depth-3 variant-A suspect set contains at most **10%** of the 2,557 functions.
* **M4:** variant B raises the depth-3 hit rate over variant A by at least **10 percentage points**.
* **M5:** the RNG-caller basis is available (an RNG difference exists) for at least **70%** of detected mutants, and its depth-3 hit rate exceeds that of the field-prior fallback.
**Also reported, not predicted:** the cross-language case (gore): the root cause p_CrossSubsector lies in the variant-A suspect set at depth 3 (57 of 923 functions within three hops). This was computed after the root cause was known and is post hoc.
**Not in scope (stated limit):** bugs in input parsing or configuration (for example room4doom's header-byte check) produce no RNG or call-sequence signature; the tool is for simulation-logic bugs.

## B. Replication of the conformance table on held-out demo seeds
New demos, generated with the registered generators: sticky 300 (seed 20261127), chaos 200 (seed 888), bot demos with seeds 6 to 11 (all maps; replay-validated). Engines as before (same builds, no changes). **Gameplay-identical** = no difference on any compared field at any tic, ignoring the view angle while the reference player is dead; the world fingerprint and keys-held mask are included where the engine has them; the cosmetic RNG index is never compared; engines whose gameplay-RNG index is not comparable are compared without it (prboom-plus, inter-doom, Kocoa).
**Predictions:** **R1:** the six C engines, doom-ascii and Managed Doom are gameplay-identical on 100% of every new set. **R2:** Mocha on at least 90% of sticky and chaos. **R3:** room4doom on fewer than 50% of sticky and chaos, and the first differing field is the weapon in at least half of its divergences on chaos. **R4:** gore and Kocoa on at most 2% of the monsters-on sets. **R5:** puredoom on at most 50% of sticky and chaos, with the weapon the first differing field in at least 80% of its divergences.

## C. Limits
Digest: the console player and a hash of the other objects and the sectors, not every field of every object. The demos are random self-play, not human play; coverage of rare code (secrets, many monsters, deathmatch, DOOM 2 content) is not claimed. Mutants are small syntactic changes, not realistic translation errors; the localiser's hit rate on real translation bugs is shown only by the one gore case. Third-party source is never committed.
