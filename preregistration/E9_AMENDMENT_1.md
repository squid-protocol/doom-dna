# `DOOM-E9` Amendment 1 (2026-11-22): reconciling two independent design reviews

Reviews: a Claude subagent and Gemini (`gemini-4-neon-high`), same brief, neither seeing the other's output. **both** = both reviews raised it. This amendment supersedes the sections of [E9_HORIZONTAL_TRANSFER.md](E9_HORIZONTAL_TRANSFER.md) it names; the rest stands. Status stays DRAFT until the registration record (Amendment 2) is committed before any E9 computation. E7 and E8/E8b results are prior knowledge (disclosed in §A).

## A. Claim, prior knowledge and wording (both)

1. **Claim is narrowed.** E9 measures **agreement with an independent source-text and git-history criterion** on same-language, near-verbatim horizontal copies, and **robustness to function renaming and reformatting** on constructed spike-ins. It cannot show that structure "finds horizontal transfer better than names" in general. The report may say a method "agrees with name-blind text copying evidence" and names its limits: names have the advantage on verbatim recall, on modules under 100 functions, and on translations (C to C++, Java to Kotlin), where text matching and structure are blind.
2. **Prior knowledge:** the `textscreen` transfers and the E7 Phase A tables (twin counts, name matches) were seen; E8 and E8b results (progenitor survival, the pins) were seen. The direction of the main comparisons is therefore expected; the informative parts are magnitudes, the stratified false-positive rates, the spike-in curve and the time checks. H9.1's stated basis is corrected: E7 showed that **names overstate code sharing across rewrites and attach from-scratch rewrites that reuse DOOM's vocabulary**; it did not show that generic names inflate matches (its generic-key rule was defective).

## B. What counts as a horizontal transfer (replaces §2 candidate and vertical definitions; both)

3. **Vertical status is defined independently of both predictors.** A function is **vertical** if the name-blind text arbiter (E7's, 4-token shingles, own name and the module prefix masked) finds a counterpart at Jaccard of at least **0.6** (`exact+near+derived`) in any **in-corpus ancestor of the port**: `linuxdoom`, and the progenitors added in E8b (Boom 2.02, MBF 2.03, SMMU, EDGE at its fork date, PrBoom 2.5.0, WinMBF), plus shared-history parents. Sensitivity: also mask functions whose E7 name key (stoplist applied) matches an ancestor's. Neither the structural twin rule nor the name rule is used to build the truth.
4. **Ancestry-clade table (fixed before scoring).** Ports whose true ancestors are outside the corpus or undeclared are placed in clades so shared clade code is not scored as transfer: **Boom/MBF/WinMBF clade** (`prboom-plus`, `dsda-doom`, `puredoom`, `woof`, `nugget-doom`, `eternity`, `edge-classic`), **Chocolate clade** (`chocolate-doom`, `crispy-doom`, `inter-doom`, `doomretro`, `doom-wasm`, `doom-ascii`, `doomgeneric`, `sdl-doom`, and the undeclared base of `sdl-doom`), **ZDoom clade** (`odamex`, `gzdoom`, whose progenitor is absent), and the undeclared pair `doom-ios`/`prboom-plus` (E7 found 272 twins). A pair of ports in the same clade is not eligible for horizontal scoring (it is reported as vertical or co-clade).
5. **Three-way classification of every confirmed near-copy pair**, tagged **before** the predictors are scored from T3 and licence headers: (i) **port-to-port transfer** (a donor port in the corpus, documented or earliest dated), (ii) **common-donor co-borrowing** (two ports that both took code from a third corpus port, for example `eternity` and `odamex` both from Chocolate's `textscreen`), (iii) **external third-party library** (a pinned list fixed before scoring: `zlib`, `libpng`, SDL, Lua, `md5`, OPL and the like, from licence headers and directory audit). The three are scored separately; `textscreen` is reported apart from the library class.
6. **Eligible port pairs:** primary scoring is restricted to **`c_family`** ports (E7's grouping), stratified `.c` with `.c` and `.c` with `.cpp`; cross-language pairs are descriptive only. `scope.csv` is applied as in E7 (it drops `src/hexen` and `src/heretic` from Chocolate and dsda-doom, so the known Hexen transfer is out of scope; a sensitivity keeps them). Histories are walked up to each port's **pinned scan SHA** so first-appearance dates align with the scanned functions.

## C. Truth sets and the time checks (replaces §2 T1-T3, §4 time checks; both)

7. **T1 (symmetric text criterion).** All eligible function pairs `(a, b)` between two ports with Jaccard at least **0.9**, computed by a global MinHash-LSH index over all functions of all pairs' ports (not one-sided, not first-hit), using **independent function boundaries** from `ctags` for the bodies. A predicted pair is a true positive iff its Jaccard is at least 0.9; recall counts covered **normalised-body equivalence classes**, so identical duplicate copies are not penalised. Co-primary sensitivities: Jaccard at least **0.6**, and an **identifier-normalised** version (callee, type and prefix tokens masked) for renamed modules. "Translated code" is struck from H9.5 (text matching cannot see it).
8. **Co-primary truth: T3 plus a hand audit.** T3 (documented, pinned file written before scoring) fixes donor and borrower for the pre-registered port-to-port transfers, flags the external libraries, and provides a module-level recall check. A **hand-audited random sample** of 50 T1-confirmed and 50 T1-unconfirmed predicted pairs, two raters, rubric fixed before the audit, gives truth that is not a text-similarity score.
9. **T2 time checks, redefined.**
   * **First appearance** of a function in a repository: walk first-parent history back from the pinned SHA, use `git diff-tree -M -C` and merge-diff inspection, and follow the function by **fuzzy body lineage** (Jaccard at least 0.8 on the T1 tokens) to the earliest introducing commit, so a post-copy edit does not move the date. **Committer date** on first-parent is primary (author date as a sensitivity).
   * **Left-censored functions** (earliest appearance within **14 days of the repository's root commit**, or introduced by a squash-import) are **excluded** from order and cluster statistics and counted per repository (`doom-wasm`, `doomgeneric`, `doom-ios`, `puredoom`, `sdl-doom` are expected to be mostly undated).
   * **Order is tested only on T3-directed port-to-port pairs**, as the **excess** donor-first share over the same pair's non-transferred random-function baseline (repository age alone makes an older donor first for most pairs). Donor and borrower are fixed from T3 before any date is read.
   * **Import cluster** uses one grouping rule for all sets: a module's matched functions in the borrower that fall within **14 days of the median**; computed identically for T1-confirmed, unconfirmed and **within-borrower function-permuted** baselines (200 seeded draws), plus a native same-directory control. Time is **supporting evidence, a falsifier not a validator**: a T1-confirmed transfer whose recipient is dated strictly before its donor, with no earlier common source, is listed and reported.
10. **Module clustering** (post hoc, never used for matching) is defined as the share of a pair's matched functions in the borrower that share a directory or lie in a weakly connected call-graph component of at least 5 functions.

## D. Predictors and baselines (replaces §2 predictors; both)

11. **Structure:** E7 twins at `fdr 0.01`, **either direction (union)**, with the structural pair set run on **full repositories and filtered post hoc** by the vertical mask (primary; the other order, pre-masked repositories, is a sensitivity because masking first can drop the candidate count below the 100 needed to certify). The exact-clone bypass for `loc` at least 12 stays as in E7.
12. **Names, made symmetric:** one-to-one pair emission by **unique key** (`n_A(k) = n_B(k) = 1`); keys occurring more than once are a sensitivity scored by body-equivalence class. The **E7 name decoy gate** (200 seeded draws, 99th percentile) is applied alongside the raw rule so both predictors face pair-level error control; `name_key_n2` (the DOOM prefix strip) is a registered sensitivity because H9.5 tests re-prefixed transfers.
13. **Random baselines:** R2 (the sfs decoys) is **removed** as a predictor (decoys are synthetic vectors, cannot be scored against pairs, and underestimate realised false hits 3 to 4 times); decoy counts are reported only as an internal calibration. R1 is kept as an explicit **chance floor**: a fixed emission budget per pair, `max(k_struct, k_names)` with a floor of 5, drawn **without replacement** within pooled `loc` deciles (empty deciles fall back to the nearest). Added: a **trivial-geometry baseline** (reciprocal nearest neighbours on `loc`, complexity and arguments alone), so structure is tested against something stronger than random. The text arbiter is reported as the **T1-equivalent ceiling** (recall near 1 by construction), not as a competitor.
14. **De-duplication:** a predictor-independent rule is **co-primary**: identical normalised bodies are collapsed within each repository before either predictor and T1 run (so `puredoom` at 99% duplicates and `mood` at 78% do not distort either method).

## E. Controls (replaces §3; both)

15. **Negative controls, stratified:** (a) **hard negatives**: same-language unrelated C/C++ programs with at least 150 eligible functions (`nginx`, `leveldb`); (b) **soft negatives**: from-scratch rewrites with at least 150 eligible functions (`helion`, `room4doom`, `rust-doom`), reported separately; (c) `quake` and `quake2` as a descriptive same-studio row; (d) `lua` and `curl`/`libuv`: **audited for vendored libraries** (Lua is vendored in `srb2`, a known external-library transfer, used as a **positive** vendoring control). Cross-language "negative" pairs are **removed**. The "0 false positives" rule is replaced by a **false-positive rate per 1,000 eligible functions with exact 95% intervals**, per stratum, for each predictor (names under the same pair-level certification).
16. **Spike-in (replaces §3 spike-in; both):**
    * **Donors** are chosen so the module is absent from the recipient and from every other corpus program: a C module from `curl` (about 150 functions) and one from `libuv` (about 50 functions). `curl` and `libuv` are therefore used as donors and are **not** used as negative controls; `nginx` and `leveldb` are.
    * **Disguise tiers:** (a) verbatim; (b) functions renamed at 50% and 100% (definitions and **call sites** updated so the code stays valid); (c) 100% renamed + comments stripped + a **pinned `clang-format` configuration**; (d) a light refactor tier (local types or wrappers changed, statements reordered where valid); (e) partial module (30%).
    * **Primary spike-in endpoint:** structure's own recall at least **0.80** under tier (c) at **both** sizes, with an exact interval. The **names-versus-structure comparison under renaming is demoted to descriptive** (names recall under 100% renaming is zero by construction). The spike-in is built into a scratch copy of a recipient port (outside the repository), searched on full repository pairs so the candidate count can clear the FDR floor; the 50-function case tests sensitivity to that floor.

## F. Hypotheses, statistics and power (replaces §4 measurement unit and §5; both)

17. **Unit:** a **transfer event** = (donor, recipient, module cluster). Shared-history families (Chocolate/Crispy/Inter, PrBoom+/dsda-doom, Woof/Nugget) are **one lineage**. Precision is **micro-averaged** (pooled over events). Uncertainty by **cluster bootstrap over lineage families** (2,000 draws, seeded), with a **leave-one-module-out** version excluding `textscreen`.
18. **Power gate:** fewer than **5 independent lineage pairs** or fewer than **2 distinct modules** makes H9.1 descriptive; counts of events, modules and functions per event are always reported. The earlier "30 T1 functions" gate is withdrawn (one `textscreen` transfer clears it).
19. **Primary endpoints (formal, with stated statistics):**
    * **P1:** the difference in micro-averaged precision (structure minus names) against the co-primary truth (T1 together with the T3 and hand-audit sets), event-clustered 95% interval; "structure higher" if the interval lies above 0.
    * **P2:** the upper 95% exact bound of the **hard-negative false-positive rate per 1,000 functions**, structure compared with names.
    Holm applies to P1 and P2 only. **Deterministic checks reported separately, not Holm-corrected:** the spike-in tier (c) recall at least 0.80; Order excess above baseline; import-cluster excess. H9.5 (what each misses, with reasons) is descriptive.
20. The earlier thresholds (precision at least 0.80; recall at least 0.8 and twice names under 100% renaming; time 80%) are **withdrawn as pass/fail rules** and replaced by the intervals and curves above.

## G. Pins and sequence (replaces §7)

* `manifest/RUN_PINS.json` (E7's 66 programs and the E8b progenitors), the ancestry-clade table, the external-library list, the T3 file, the spike-in donors and recipes and the `clang-format` configuration are committed **before** any truth set or predictor is computed.
* Order: (1) register by commit; (2) compute and **record the T1 truth-set size** (events, modules, functions) before scoring any predictor; (3) run predictors and controls; commit output manifest hashes; (4) analysis and report. Code and tests are committed before step 2 (a dated relaxation of "before any computation" applies only to the outcome-blind truth builder, as in E8).
