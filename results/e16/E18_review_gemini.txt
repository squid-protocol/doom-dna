### Prioritised Design Review: [E18_DOOMGRAPH.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E18_DOOMGRAPH.md) & [SCHEMA.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/doomgraph/SCHEMA.md)

#### 1. [P0] P1 collapses to a 3-port faithful-clone lookup, is post-hoc, and has an unsatisfiable threshold
* **Defects:** Only 3 of ~36 ports have lesion screens (`chocolate-doom`, `managed-doom`, `prboom-plus`). Two are C forks with identical/near-identical code; the third is a faithful 1:1 port. Leave-one-port-out therefore leaks shared code across folds and never tests a multi-lineage 36-port graph (~33 ports add no phenotypes). Worse, E15/E16 outcomes for all three ports are already read, so P1 is not blind. Finally, E15 A4 showed `name-only` and `N0` call-count profiles already reach ~95–100% accuracy on these faithful ports—making P1's pass rule (**+30 percentage points over the best baseline**) mathematically impossible (>100%).
* **Changes:** Disclose 3-port P1 as a retrospective development benchmark; defer confirmatory P1 until blind divergent ports (Mocha/gore post-E17) are added. Exclude identical/near-identical text pairs (`chocolate-doom`–`prboom-plus`, per E16 T2), stratify results on **non-name-agree / divergent** pairs where baselines fail, require `leave-lineage-out` for confirmatory claims, and replace "+30pp" with a simulation-calibrated lift over the best non-oracle baseline.

#### 2. [P0] Leakage and circularity in P1, P2, P3, and group construction
* **Defects:**
  * *P3:* Hiding 10% of `match_edge`s leaks directly unless `ortholog_group` is rebuilt from scratch and *all* parallel edges on hidden pairs (`name-agree`, E7 text-arbiter, sibling E12 tiers) are removed; 2-hop clone paths still trivially recover edges.
  * *P2:* `behaviour_confirmed` embeds `identical call profile` alongside E15 `D <= p05`, exists only on the 3 faithful ports (leave-one-port-out leaves **one** port-pair to train on), compares against `sfs_confidence` calibrated on the same ports, and leaks if group features include the target edge.
  * *P1:* Importing the held-out port's E15 `hot/inert`/`usable` mask leaks outcomes into candidate filtering; "call-graph neighbours" is ambiguous (the held-out port's own phenotypes cannot be used). Neither document enforces that groups never read phenotype/reach layers.
* **Changes:** Enforce a strict static-only build boundary for `ortholog_group` (no `phenotype`, `demo_reach`, or `behaviour_confirmed`). Rebuild groups inside each P2/P3 fold with all parallel edges on held-out pairs stripped. In P2, evaluate `D`-only confirmation separately from call-profile identity and treat 3-port P2 as descriptive. In P1, predict `hot/inert` without held-out masks and use only cross-port mapped neighbours.

#### 3. [P1] P1 metric conflation, missing baselines, clustering, and multiplicity
* **Defects:** P1 conflates E15 function-level `D` with "lesion-effect family profile" (distinct objects in E15/E17), leaves `D` undefined for `inert` functions, lacks rules for multi-member/flagged/ungrouped functions, and proposes "top-5 within group" when groups contain ~1–3 screened instances. Baselines omit **direct pairwise E12 matches (no graph)**, **ortholog-only vs neighbour-only ablations**, **same-file/subsystem**, and **size/reach-matched** priors. Clustering solely on `ortholog_group` ignores repeated scoring of each group across 3 ports and cross-group dependence via call-graph neighbours/shared demos; P2/P3 lack endpoint clustering; no multiplicity control covers 4 P1 metrics, 6 P2 tiers, or P3 hit@k.
* **Changes:** Fix one primary P1 scalar (macro-averaged function `D <= E15 p05`, scoring ungrouped functions as misses), specify aggregation and `hot/inert` handling, add the 4 missing baselines/ablations, cluster-bootstrap by **subsystem/file** (P1) and **edge endpoints** (P2/P3), and apply Holm correction across secondary tests/tiers.

#### 4. [P1] Underspecified ortholog-group construction rules
* **Defects:** [SCHEMA.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/doomgraph/SCHEMA.md) lists 5 reference engines but anchors on `linuxdoom` or `chocolate-doom` (anchoring on `chocolate-doom` privileges a P1 test port). Score unification across `F0/F1c/S/ext/name-agree/E7`, max path length, port-order determinism, 1:N splits, N:1 merges, non-anchor extensions, and scoring of flagged conflicts are unfrozen.
* **Changes:** Freeze a deterministic clustering spec by commit before any scoring: anchor on `linuxdoom` (non-test), define edge weights/transitivity/tie-breakers, encode `split`/`merge`/`conflict` roles explicitly, and define how multi-group and port-only functions enter evaluation.

#### 5. [P1] Vague uninterpretable criteria and missing controls
* **Defects:** "Membership changing >10%" under anchor swap lacks a metric/scope and will trigger on legitimate `linuxdoom`-vs-`chocolate-doom` diffs; "carried by one subsystem" has no rule; unstratified shuffles ignore size/reach/subsystem confounding; "hold-out of a different layer" is undefined.
* **Changes:** Quantify uninterpretable gates: leave-one-subsystem-out failure, shared-function anchor-swap ARI < 0.85, ungrouped rate > 30%, missing-`D` > 40%, or baseline > 70% (ceiling). Add **size/reach/subsystem-stratified shuffles**, a **true-negative (no-ortholog) control**, and a **canary null-label leakage audit**.

#### 6. [P2] Schema integrity, reproducibility, and third-party code leakage
* **Defects:** Tables lack PK/FK constraints, surrogate `edge_id` (needed by `behaviour_confirmed`/`sfs_confidence`), canonical undirected edge ordering, `build_manifest`, promised `source`/`build_id` columns, `demo` (IWAD/family), `match_edge.gate_passed`, `membership` conflict flags/`build_scope`, `lesion` site-reach/exclusion flags, `phenotype.demo_set_id`, `sfs_confidence` formula/version, and decomposed `behaviour_confirmed` fields (`D`, threshold, call-profile flag). `instance.fid` breaks on re-scans/overloads; `port.lineage_parent` duplicates `lineage_edge`. Free-text `evidence`/`source` (especially E7 text-arbiter rationales or `gene-view` snippets) and raw `scan_path` risk leaking third-party source code.
* **Changes:** Add PK/FKs, `edge_id`, `build_manifest`, `demo`, `build_scope`, missing flags/provenance columns, and signature-hashed instance keys. Restrict `evidence` to structured IDs/enums (no free-text snippets/rationales), store relative/hashed `scan_path`, and enforce an automated ingest allowlist.
