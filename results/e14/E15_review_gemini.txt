Prioritised methodological and statistical review of [E15_ROLE_SCREEN.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E15_ROLE_SCREEN.md) (checked against [schemata.py](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/demo/harness/mscreen/schemata.py), [runner.py](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/demo/harness/mscreen/runner.py), [patch_abort.py](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/demo/harness/mscreen/patch_abort.py), [E14_SAME_BREAK_SAME_ROLE.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E14_SAME_BREAK_SAME_ROLE.md), and [E13_DEMO_CONFORMANCE.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E13_DEMO_CONFORMANCE.md)).

### Priority 0 — Blocking Code, Definition, and Circularity Defects

1. **Unit-of-analysis contradiction breaks H1–H3.** Analysis rules designate the *function* as the independent unit, yet H1–H3 cluster *individual lesions*. Within-function lesion dependence and unequal function sizes (hot functions have 30–80+ lesions) make lesion-level gap statistics trivially reject independent nulls, let a few large functions satisfy ">=70% of lesions in stable groups," and leave host-function membership undefined when a function's lesions split across clusters—breaking H3's partition-requiring NMI and creating overlapping H2 groups.
   - *Fix:* Make **function-level clustering** primary (one aggregated phenotype per function with >=4 non-silent lesions); treat lesion-level clustering as a secondary check for split/multi-role functions.

2. **Including `reach profile` in Function Phenotype makes PC1 tautological and confounds H2–H4/PC4.** Clean entry counts belong to the function, not a lesion half-split; including them forces PC1 split-half matching near 100% even on noise, and inflates H2 (callers/callees co-enter), PC4 (file co-entry), and H3–H4 (100%-conformant ports share identical clean reach). Passing H1–H4 via clean reach alone is scientifically uninterpretable.
   - *Fix:* Remove clean reach from the phenotype vector. Add **N0 (clean-reach-only baseline)**—clustering and nearest-neighbour matching on clean entry counts alone—and require H1–H4 and PC4 to beat N0.

3. **Pipeline/registration mismatches invalidate state classes, strict mode, and C# reach.**
   - [patch_abort.py](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/demo/harness/mscreen/patch_abort.py) checks `E13_PRND` (col 2) *before* player/monster/sector/key columns, hardcodes dead-angle skipping, and aborts on first mismatch. Thus: (a) strict mode is **not stored** (contradicting E15); (b) RNG masks downstream state classes on most kills; (c) `prboom-plus` (PC2) lacks `prndindex`, breaking column comparability.
   - [runner.py](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/demo/harness/mscreen/runner.py) maps `fid_of[(file, name)]`, colliding on C# overloaded methods/constructors and silently misassigning reach profiles; it also outputs `col = -1` for row-count mismatches, has no timeout retry (unlike E13), and leaves crash/hang handling out of similarity.
   - *Fix:* Exclude `prndindex` from cross-port state-class scoring (or re-score without col 2); key `fid_of` by `(file, name, line)`/`fid`; specify `col=-1` and crash/hang coding; drop the "strict stored" claim; retry timeouts once.

4. **Undefined distance metrics, omitted first-divergence tic, and severity/0/0 collapse.**
   - Lesion similarity lacks weights for "first-class agreement"; function-to-function distance for H4/H5/PC1–PC3 is completely unspecified.
   - **First-divergence tic** is recorded (`results.tic`) yet omitted from similarity and function phenotypes. Without it, core/hot functions killing ~42/42 demos collapse into one ties-ridden mega-cluster, destroying H1–H4 and causing PC1 top-1 tie failures.
   - Pairwise restriction to mutually reached demos still yields **0/0 (`NaN`)** for disjoint-reach or zero-kill-on-overlap pairs, inflates Jaccard=1.0 on 1–2 shared demos, and violates triangle inequality for average-linkage clustering (which also suffers from singleton outliers).
   - *Fix:* Freeze an explicit function-distance metric combining per-demo kill rate, first-divergence tic (e.g., log-tic or rank agreement on co-killed demos), and non-RNG state class, with explicit unreached/missing handling and shrinkage or minimum-overlap rules.

### Priority 1 — Major Null, Clustering, and Cross-Port Defects

5. **Unspecified gap null, fragile demo bootstrap, and unjustified `k in [6, 25]`.** E15 cites a "registered null below" for the gap statistic, but none is defined; uniform bounding-box nulls are invalid for sparse reach-masked data. Bootstrapping 42 heterogeneous demos (20 sticky, 10 chaos, 12 monster-free bot) destabilises niche subsystems reached by few demos. On ~110–140 reached functions, `k=25` averages ~5 functions/group, starving H2's `>=5`-host filter.
   - *Fix:* Define the gap null via **within-demo-stratum permutation conditioned on each function's reach mask** (plus N0 comparison); stratify demo bootstrap by demo family with Hennig max-Jaccard matching; replace the rigid `k=6..25` band with a coverage-scaled bound.

6. **H2 call-graph nulls and same-file test are biased.** Same-file random draws cannot be beaten for small files (`p_telept.c`, `p_doors.c`, etc., where file size $\approx$ group size). Unconnected function-pointer thinkers/callbacks (`A_*`, `T_*`, `PTR_*`) saturate the distance cap (6). Sampling/rewiring (N1/N2) over the full 2,431-function graph rather than the ~130 screened gameplay functions makes H2 trivially pass.
   - *Fix:* Restrict N1/N2 to the **induced subgraph of screened simulation functions**; test **cross-file host pairs only** against size/reach-matched cross-file pairs to isolate non-file locality; report H2 stratified by statically connected vs thinker/callback functions.

7. **H3 recurrence tests are anti-conservative and asymmetric.** Demo-label permutation (N3) destroys shared monster-vs-bot and reach structure, making centroid matching trivially pass. Raw NMI inflates with `k` and imbalance; mutual-best-match `>=70%` is algebraically impossible if `k_md < 0.7 * k_ch`.
   - *Fix:* Test H3 centroid matching via **function-label permutation within reach strata** (and against N0); replace NMI with **AMI/ARI**; freeze the C-to-C# name-agree rule; normalize mutual-best-match by `min(k_ch, k_md)`.

### Priority 2 — Statistics, Multiplicity, and Controls

8. **Primary-test contradictions and missing power/asymmetry rules.** Analysis rules specify "Holm over the five primaries" while naming three (H2, H3, H4), giving H1 no single p-value and marking H5 exploratory. H2's "share of groups at FDR 0.05" lacks an aggregate test p-value. No minimum sample size handles asymmetric port screens or high silent/unreached-branch rates (note: function-level reach conflates unreached branches with equivalent mutants; also [schemata.py](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/demo/harness/mscreen/schemata.py) already drops C array-index and C# bracketed-arg mutations, so "bounds lesions" should be revised to shift/signed-overflow UB).
   - *Fix:* Register exactly **4 confirmatory primaries** (H1–H4) with one scalar test and Holm adjustment (H2 primary: permutation p-value of mean within-group call distance); keep H5 exploratory with an equivalence CI; set minimum-N thresholds (e.g., >=60 eligible functions/port, >=5 qualifying H2 groups) below which results are *inconclusive for power*.

9. **Control gaps.** **PC1:** require `>=8 non-silent lesions` (4/half), exclude clean reach, break ties with first-tic. **PC2:** `prboom-plus`/`crispy-doom` are unscreened and inherit C source—clarify whether PC2 gates E15 now and drop `prndindex`. **PC3:** cites "12" E14 confirmatory agree functions (E14 sampled 15—possible leakage/pilot mix-up) and overlaps H4; drop PC3 or exclude it from H4. **PC5:** run on both ports. **N4/N5:** freeze C-to-C# file mapping for N4; note N5 (disagree) is not a pure negative control.

### Priority 3 — Missing High-Value Analyses (Exploratory)

10. **Add:** (a) **dynamic caller–callee graph** (not merely co-entry) to recover thinker/callback neighbourhoods; (b) **E12 homology & split/merged roles** (enrichment of E12 tiers in phenotype groups; top-k/soft matching for split C# methods); (c) **demo-family stratification** (monster-on vs bot) to rule out a binary monster/movement artifact; (d) **causal tic ordering** along call edges.
