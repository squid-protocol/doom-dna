**Prioritised Design Review of [E16_SYMPTOM_DIAGNOSIS.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E16_SYMPTOM_DIAGNOSIS.md)**

### Critical (fix before freezing)

1. **Observation asymmetry and reach leakage in the scorer (Area 2).** Dictionary lesions treat unexecuted demos as "unobserved", whereas a real bug yields `killed`/`same` over *all* runnable demos (its execution mask is unknown). Scoring only co-observed demos creates two flaws: (i) a rare candidate executing on 5 of a bug's 40 failing demos gets Jaccard = 1.0 despite missing the other 35; (ii) masking T1/T2 test lesions to their executed demos leaks fault reach.
   **Fix:** Evaluate dictionary and test vectors over all demos run on the test port ($D_{\text{port}}$: 77 for T1/T2; runnable shareware demos for gore/Kocoa), coding unexecuted dictionary sites as `same`. Treat only unrunnable demos and crashes/hangs as missing; require $\ge 5$ evaluated demos and $\ge 1$ kill in $D_{\text{port}}$.

2. **Dev/test leakage, E15 disclosure, and threshold revision (Areas 1, 4).** Tuning on ~70 dev functions while testing on 140 either leaks test-half dictionary entries into weights (if dev searches all 140) or halves the pool (chance 7.1% vs 3.6%), miscalibrating A1's 0.40. Also, "140 usable" uses seen E15 outcomes (hot/inert masks), and unrestricted downward revision of 0.40 after dev-half undermines falsifiability.
   **Fix:** Tune strictly on dev queries vs dev dictionary using a file-stratified split. Disclose E16 as a secondary analysis of existing E15 screens with [E15_ROLE_SCREEN.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E15_ROLE_SCREEN.md) hot/inert masks and shift-lesion exclusions frozen verbatim. Replace open revision with a viability floor (stop/null if dev-half fails `B2 + 0.10`; fix T1's floor at `max(0.25, B2 + 0.15)` pre-unblinding).

3. **T1/T2 proxy circularity and counterpart bias (Area 1).** Applying identical one-token operators to a line-by-line port (managed-doom) or shared C lineage (prboom-plus, including identical-text functions) tests cross-port replay of the *same perturbation*, not organic bugs. Requiring "identical call profile on all 77 demos" (clarify binary vs counts) cherry-picks faithful functions where clean coverage (B2) already matches near-perfectly (E15 A4) and excludes refactored/split functions where real bugs live.
   **Fix:** Frame T1/T2 as synthetic one-token transfer across faithful ports; exclude identical-text functions from primary T2 (report as upper bound); add a **leave-operator-class-out** ablation (e.g., diagnose `sdl` bugs using non-`sdl` dictionary lesions) and a name-agree sensitivity without identical-profile filtering.

### Major (methodological & statistical defects)

4. **Undefined scorer terms, `time` class, and size bias (Areas 2, 6).** Components (b) class-set and (c) rank-based tic closeness are undefined when mutually killed demos = 0 (and within-lesion tic rank degenerates for 1–2 demos). E16 adds a `time` class absent from E15 (`player, world, sector, keys`). `max` aggregation induces extreme-value bias favoring large functions with many dictionary lesions; capping *test* lesions at 5 leaves dictionary-side size bias intact. Crash/hang rules are omitted.
   **Fix:** Compute (b) (per-demo Jaccard of E15 class bits; drop or define `time`) and (c) (E15's scaled $|\Delta\log(\text{tic}+1)|$) on mutually killed demos, setting both to 0 when empty (never renormalize). Exclude crashes/hangs per E15; deduplicate identical/subsumed dictionary kill vectors for the top-3 sensitivity.

5. **Baseline flaws and missing comparators (Area 3).** A1 cites "B2's top-5", but B2 lists both Ochiai and Tarantula; with 0 passing demos (likely on monsters-on sets for gore/Kocoa), Tarantula divides by zero and Ochiai collapses to B4. Filtering hot/inert from B2 leaks E15 lesion outcomes into standalone SBFL. Missing: binary-kill-only **Metallaxis-FL** (isolating tic+class value), a **dictionary lesion-count (size) prior**, and an A2 baseline (15% chance and [E13_DEMO_CONFORMANCE.md](file:///srv/storage_16tb/projects/doom-dna-worktrees/e13-demo-pilot/preregistration/E13_DEMO_CONFORMANCE.md)'s `FIELD_FAMILY`). B3 (E13) is honestly contextualised.
   **Fix:** Choose Ochiai (with safe 0-pass handling) as primary B2, report B2 on both usable and unfiltered entered pools, and add Metallaxis-only and lesion-count baselines.

6. **Inference, clustering, power, multiplicity, and A2 (Area 4).** Comparing the dictionary's CI lower bound to B2's point estimate ignores paired covariance and omits $\alpha$. Subsampling $\le 5$ lesions adds avoidable variance. Pooled A1b must cluster by **chocolate-doom counterpart function** (avoiding cross-engine double-counting). Only ~30–45 test-half counterparts may qualify, risking underpowering. A2's "severity band", scorer, and pessimistic ties (buckets >15% break top-15%) are undefined.
   **Fix:** Test A1 via a **paired cluster-bootstrap 95% CI** on $\Delta = \text{Top5}_{\text{dict}} - \text{Top5}_{\text{B2}}$ (lower bound $>0$, point lift $\ge 0.15$). Replace 5-lesion subsampling with exact equal-function weighting ($1/m_f$). Make A1 sole confirmatory (A1b/A2 Holm-adjusted secondary; A3/A4 descriptive), add a power gate ($\ge 30$ test-half functions, else "inconclusive"), and freeze A2's rule.

7. **Real cases gore and Kocoa ($n=2$) (Area 5).** `P_CrossSubsector` (`p_sight.c`) may be excluded as hot/inert, making A3 impossible or shifting truth to unbounded "immediate callees". A3 is retrospective; A4's 5-function set has ~31% top-10 chance in 140 candidates (not 3%) and may fall outside the 140 usable functions. "Where gore diverges and chocolate-doom does not" is garbled—passing demos are required, yet E13 showed gore/Kocoa pass mainly on bot demos, letting a monsters-on-vs-bot shortcut rank monster functions high.
   **Fix:** Label A3 retrospective and A4 prospective ($n=2$, descriptive). Record `P_CrossSubsector`'s usable/hot status pre-run; score it alone separately from 1-hop G3 callees with set-adjusted chance; score out-of-universe causes as misses. Verify matched demo lists, non-RNG/`--ignore-dead-angle` column parity, and repeat-run determinism on regenerated digests.

### Moderate (interpretability & hygiene)

8. **Uninterpretable outcomes and controls (Area 6).** Pre-declare uninterpretable results: failing to beat B2, lesion-count prior, or a demo-family-only (monsters-on vs bot / shareware vs Freedoom) predictor; near-hot dominance; or self-diagnosis ceiling below A1's threshold. Clarify whether self-diagnosis splits demos per lesion or equals T3, and report tie-set sizes plus the T1/T2 drop rate from the $\ge 3$-demo filter.
